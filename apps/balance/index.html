BTC Balance History

See every transaction in a Bitcoin wallet and the balance right after each one, in USD and BTC. Useful when a balance changed after a Resync and you want to see where the funds went.

In Edge, open your Bitcoin wallet, tap the ⋮ menu, choose View XPub Address, and copy it here.

Your zpub lets anyone view this wallet's transaction history, but it can't move funds. It goes only to the Bitcoin server you choose (an Edge server by default) and is never saved. Edge will never ask for your recovery phrase (seed words).
